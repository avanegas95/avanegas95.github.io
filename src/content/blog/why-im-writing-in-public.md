I've spent years making software more reliable — manual testing first, then automation, CI pipelines, and mobile tooling. Most of that work stays inside repos and standups. This blog is a place to **write down what I learn** so future-me (and maybe you) can find it later.

## What you'll find here

- **Engineering notes** — automation patterns, Python helpers, CI/CD lessons, and QA mindset without pretending every problem has one right answer.
- **Things I've implemented** — small tools and workflows that saved time, with enough detail to be useful, not enough to put anyone's employer on blast.
- **Creative side projects** — videography, photography, and editing experiments that keep me curious outside of work.

## What you won't find

- Confidential product details or proprietary code.
- Hot takes dressed up as expertise.
- A publishing schedule I can't keep — I'd rather post something useful every few weeks than ship filler weekly.

## Why on avanegas.com

I want one home for my portfolio and my writing. If a post helps someone on Dev.to later, great — but the canonical version lives here.

Thanks for reading. More soon.
