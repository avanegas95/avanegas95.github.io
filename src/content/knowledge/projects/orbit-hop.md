### What it does

Astro Andy: Orbit Hop is a one-tap arcade game for iPhone. Andy orbits a planet. A tap launches him toward the next one. Launching when the guide lights up gives a Perfect, which earns extra Stardust and builds a combo. Miss every orbit and the run ends. Stardust unlocks new pilots and trails.

### Highlights

- Progress is saved on the iPhone and, with iCloud on, copied to the player's iCloud account.
- Scores and achievements go to Game Center for signed-in players.
- The only ad is optional. Players can watch one to double a run's Stardust. There are no pop-up ads. A Supporter Pack gives double Stardust without ads.
- There are no accounts and no personal data is sent to Anderson. Ads are non-personalized.

### QA angle

The game has a public support page and a privacy policy on this site. Players can report bugs at avanegas95@gmail.com, and replies usually arrive within a few days. The support page also covers restoring purchases and moving to a new phone.
