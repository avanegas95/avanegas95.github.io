When I first started my career, I had no idea SQA was even a job someone could have. I happened upon it — and loved the idea of improving software through the eyes of a customer. Since then I've grown in manual testing, picked up automation, stumbled through CI testing, and with AI in the mix, taken on projects I never would have tried before.

These posts serve a few purposes. I want to **write down what I learn** so future-me (and maybe you) can find it, refer to it, and look back on it later.

But I don't want to limit myself to work stuff. I could stay focused on just the technical bits, but I have other hobbies and interests, and I want to share those too. Who knows — maybe we overlap on a few!

## What you'll find here

- **Engineering notes** — tools I use, personal experiences, the mindset I bring to work, and general thoughts and opinions.
- **Side projects** — things I've built, how I implemented them, my workflows, timesavers, tools used, hurdles I've overcome, fears I've conquered.
- **Hobbies** — videography, photography, gaming, restaurants, experiences, travel. There's a lot out there to explore, and lessons from outside work often sneak back into how I think about engineering.

## What you won't find

- Fake expertise — there are many ways to solve a problem, many tools coming out, many mindsets worth adopting.
- Confidential company information — I like my job and want to keep it. Anything I work on for personal projects, I'll gladly show and tell.
- A regular publishing cadence — I'd rather publish something meaningful than ship filler.

## Why on avanegas.com

I want one home for my portfolio and my writing. Maybe this isn't the perfect spot for it, but figuring that out is part of the learning experience too.
